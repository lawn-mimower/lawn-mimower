### 🚀 Shipped & Built

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

#### [🏛️ LawMaster](https://github.com/lawn-mimower/lawmaster)
`Python` `FastAPI` `LightRAG` `Mistral OCR`

Retrieval over Indian industrial & environmental law — **43 statutes, 1,210 pages**, much of it Hindi.

Uniform chunking destroys legal text, so a router splits the corpus four ways — definitions, sections, tables, amendments — each to the store that fits it. Answers cite back to the source page.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

#### [🧪 Luna Reasoning Eval](https://github.com/lawn-mimower/luna-reasoning-eval)
`Python` `OpenAI` `BFCL`

How much reasoning does a task actually need? **11 suites, 4 effort modes, ~9,000 API calls**, a cost ledger on every one.

For most tasks, maximum effort bought nothing measurable. Ships with an adversarial audit of its own conclusions.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

#### [🏗️ ForeSites](https://github.com/lawn-mimower/foresites)
`Node` `React` `Python` `AWS Lambda`

Construction snag tracking over **WhatsApp** — site staff report defects with a photo or voice note.

Lands in Postgres, gets assigned, tracked to a signed-off resolution. Four subsystems, including a Lambda agent turning plain English into SQL and a chart.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

#### [👁️ Stereo Vergence Scanner](https://github.com/lawn-mimower/stereo-vergence-scanner)
`C++` `Python` `ESP8266`

2D room mapping on **~£15 of parts**. Two ToF sensors on two servos, verging on a point like a pair of eyes.

A single sweeping sensor can't tell a wall from the edge of a doorway. Two verged ones can.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

#### [📐 IMU Drift Compensation](https://github.com/lawn-mimower/imu-drift-compensation)
`C++` `Python` `scikit-learn`

Measuring gyro drift instead of assuming it. Fits the drift of a stationary MPU6050 and subtracts it live.

The correction comes from the sensor's own behaviour, not a datasheet constant.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

#### 🌾 AgroSense
`Research`

Modality substitution in low-cost precision agriculture — review paper on replacing expensive sensing with cheaper proxies.

<sub>*Paper, not a repo.*</sub>
